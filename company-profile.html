About Royal Infra Projects

From civil contracting to large-scale electrical infrastructure.

Royal Infra Projects began in 2024 executing civil works for utility and infrastructure contractors. Consistent delivery, competitive project economics and a willingness to take on the groundwork others avoided opened access to larger electrical packages.

The company now executes complete electrical scope — HT/LT network construction, RMU/DTR/FSP infrastructure, HDD, terminations, earthing and dismantling — alongside the civil work it originated in. That combination removes the interface gap where distribution projects typically lose time and accountability.

Growth has been matched by structure: procurement controls, inventory systems, measurement and billing discipline, and documented project management processes now govern execution across concurrent sites.

How We Execute

A defined sequence, applied to every package

The same eight stages govern a single feeder and a multi-location programme.

  1. 01

    Survey

    Route walked, existing services detected and site constraints recorded before design is fixed.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Scheme, sequence and work fronts agreed against the client programme.

  3. 03

    Procure

    Materials sourced to specification with rates and delivery locked to the schedule.

  4. 04

    Mobilise

    Crews, plant and site infrastructure deployed to the approved front.

  5. 05

    Execute

    Civil and electrical scope carried out to drawing under permit-to-work discipline.

  6. 06

    Verify

    Testing, inspection and quality checks completed before energisation.

  7. 07

    Bill

    Joint measurement, reconciliation and billing supported by full documentation.

  8. 08

    Handover

    As-built records, test reports and asset details issued to the client.
