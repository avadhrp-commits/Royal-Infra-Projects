About Us

From a civil contracting enterprise
to a structured EPC organisation

Founded in April 2024 on a construction site in Bavla, Royal Infra Projects grew from a small civil contracting crew into a certified electrical infrastructure execution company — one project, one client relationship at a time.

Today we design, build and commission 11 kV HT/LT networks, overhead-to-underground conversions, underground cable systems and substation infrastructure across Gujarat — backed by ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certification for quality, environmental and occupational health & safety management. Every project runs on the same discipline: eco-conscious execution, rigorous documentation, and a crew that treats a handover date like a promise.

Infrastructure for a nation in motion — wider networks, safer systems, and reliable power reaching every home and business they touch.
